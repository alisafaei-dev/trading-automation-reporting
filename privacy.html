Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

About This Application

Trading Automation Reporting is a personal desktop application used to back up and verify research and paper-trading reports.

Google Drive Access

The application requests the Google Drive metadata.readonly permission to identify files and compare their sizes and available checksums against locally generated backup manifests.

The permission allows reading file metadata across the authorized Google Drive account, although the application is configured to search only its designated reporting folder.

The application does not use this permission to download, modify, upload, or delete Google Drive files. Uploads are handled separately by Google Drive for desktop.

Data Storage and Sharing

Backup files, reports, and authorization tokens are managed locally or through the account owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are not published on this website.

The application is intended for personal use. It does not operate a public user-data collection service or sell Google user data.

Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner controls locally stored files and files synchronized to Google Drive, including their deletion and retention.

Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings under Third-party connections.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
